This Privacy Policy applies to Luna's Pet on the Apple App Store, on Google Play, and on the website https://lunas-pet.web.app. The same policy is used for the apps and the website.

1. Introduction

Luna's Pet (“the App” and “the Website”) is a pet-care application operated by Jesus Lujan Goebel. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it. By using the App or the Website you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

Account data: email and password (or sign-in provider), display name, and family PIN invitations.

Profile and household: address, phone, and ZIP used for your account and to find nearby clinics. Address, email, and phone are private and are not shown on maps or in Luna's Channel.

Pet records: name, photos, weight, notes, vaccines, medications, and documents you upload.

Luna's Channel: posts, comments, likes, and friend connections you choose to create.

Device and usage: app version, device type, crash or diagnostic data if enabled, and notification tokens for reminders.

Location (optional): only if you allow it, for maps or Find My Pet style features.

Payments: if you subscribe, Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store) process the payment. We do not receive or store your full card number. You manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

3. Photos, camera, and AI

You may photograph vaccine labels, prescriptions, or reports. Those images are stored with your pet file. If you use the scan feature, the image may be sent to Google Gemini so the App can read and translate the text. Use that feature only with documents you are allowed to process.

4. How we use information

To run your account, sync pet records, send reminders you set, show nearby services, operate Luna's Channel, process subscriptions, fix bugs, and respond to support at petfamilyluna@gmail.com.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with providers needed to run the App (cloud hosting, sign-in, maps, Apple or Google billing, and Gemini when you scan a document), with family members you invite, or if the law requires it. Channel posts are visible to other users according to that feature’s settings.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep account and pet data while the account is open. You can delete your account in Settings. After deletion we remove or de-identify personal data except records we must keep by law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted connections. No method of storage is 100% secure.

9. Your choices

You can decline location, camera, or notifications in device settings. You can edit or delete pet records in the App. For access or deletion requests: petfamilyluna@gmail.com.

10. International users

The App may be used outside the United States. Data may be processed in the United States.

11. Third-party links

The App may link to veterinary sites or stores. Their privacy practices are their own.

12. Not a veterinary service

Luna's Pet is a personal organizer. It is not a clinic, not a veterinarian, and not emergency care. AI text from scans can be wrong. Confirm medicines and doses with a licensed veterinarian.

13. California / similar rights

We do not sell personal information. You may request to know, correct, or delete personal information by emailing petfamilyluna@gmail.com.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
